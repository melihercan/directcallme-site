DirectCallMe

Private video calls, chat and file transfer between two devices. No server, no account, no phone number.

Need help? Write to melihercan@gmail.com. Say which device you use and what happened, and we will get back to you.

How a call starts

  1. One person taps Start a call and shares the invitation, as a QR code or a link.
  2. The other person opens the link, or taps Join a call and scans the QR code, and sends back the reply the same way.
  3. The first person scans or pastes the reply, and the call connects directly between the two devices.

A QR code shown in person is the most private way: no copy of the invitation is left anywhere.

Common questions

The call does not connect

Both devices need to be online, with the camera, microphone and (on Apple devices) local network permissions allowed. Some mobile networks block direct connections from both ends at once. If that happens, try again with one of the two devices on Wi-Fi.

How much does it cost?

The first five calls are free. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited calls. It is not a subscription. On a new device, tap Restore a previous purchase while signed in to the same store account.

Is my call recorded or stored anywhere?

No. The call goes directly between the two devices, encrypted, and nothing about it is saved. See the privacy policy for exactly what does and does not leave your device.
