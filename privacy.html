DirectCallMe Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

In short: DirectCallMe has no server, no account and no analytics. A call, its chat and its files go directly between your device and the other person's device, encrypted. The developer never receives your calls, your messages, your contacts or anything that identifies you, because the app has nowhere to send them.

Who is responsible

DirectCallMe is made by Melih Ercan, an individual developer in Türkiye ("we"). You can reach us at melihercan@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no third-party library that collects data. We have no server that could store anything about you, and we keep no record of who calls whom or when.

What stays on your device

  • Your display name. By default this is your device's name (for example "Anna's iPhone"). You can change it in Settings.
  • Your settings: low-data mode, LAN-only mode and the list of STUN or TURN servers.
  • The free-call counter, a single number. It is kept in the device's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices, which can keep it after the app is removed) so that reinstalling does not reset the trial.

Nothing about a call is written to disk. A file you receive is kept only if you choose to save it, and then only where you save it.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • To the person you call: your camera video, microphone audio, chat messages, the files you choose to send and your display name. These travel directly between the two devices and are encrypted with WebRTC's standard encryption (DTLS-SRTP), using keys that exist only for that call.
  • The invitation and the reply. To start a call, the two apps exchange a short code, shown as a QR code or shared as a link. It contains the network addresses your device can be reached at and the details needed to set up the encrypted connection. You choose how to hand it over. A QR code shown in person leaves no copy anywhere. A code sent through a messaging app stays in that app and on its servers, under that app's own privacy policy.
  • To a STUN server, once per call: a request that lets your device learn its own public network address, so that the other device can reach it. The server sees your IP address and the time of the request, and nothing else. It learns nothing about who you are calling. By default the app asks Google (stun.l.google.com) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com), whose handling of that request is covered by their own privacy policies. You can replace them with any server you prefer, or turn on LAN-only mode so that no request is sent at all.
  • To a TURN server, only if you add one. The app uses none by default. If you add one in Settings, and a direct connection cannot be made, the call is relayed through that server. It stays encrypted, but the server can see both devices' IP addresses and how much data passes through.
  • To your app store, for the one-time unlock. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, Google or Microsoft. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether this product has been bought on your store account.

As with any internet connection, your internet provider and the networks along the way can see that two addresses are exchanging data. They cannot see what is said.

Permissions

  • Camera: to send your video, and to scan an invitation QR code. The QR code is read on your device.
  • Microphone: to send your voice.
  • Local network (Apple devices): to connect directly to a device on the same network.

The camera and microphone are used only during a call, or while you are scanning a QR code.

Information from the app stores

Apple, Google and Microsoft give developers summary statistics such as download numbers, and crash reports from users who allowed their device to share them. These do not identify you to us, and they are governed by the store's own privacy policy.

Children

DirectCallMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK give you the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. To remove everything the app keeps on your device, delete the app. To clear the free-call counter from an Apple device's Keychain as well, you can reset the device. Questions are welcome at the address above.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
